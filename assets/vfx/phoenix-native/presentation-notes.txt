Original JW bird: miHoYo / HoYoverse artwork and skeletal animation.
Reference: official Phoenix demonstration
https://bh3.mihoyo.com/gl/character/detail/41

Sequence: human preparation, transformation, ascent, wings, dive, human landing.
The performer is hidden only during the bird phase (0.30 to 2.10 seconds).
Both visibility and the bird use combat's clock, including pause and hit-stop.
The first damage contact is still 2.10; the second impact is still 2.82.

flight.json is sampled from the original scene by tools/bake_phoenix_flight.gd.
It contains original root positions and actual skinned-geometry bounds at 60 Hz.
These lightweight samples frame the bird; no per-frame vertex scan is required.
The old implementation canceled all root movement and kept the bird at y=2.2.
The current implementation retains the authored ascent, dive and all bone poses.

Side-view adaptation: uniform bird scale .65, source .10–1.916667 fits the
0.30–2.10 combat interval, forward depth is compressed into the duel lane.
The bird emerges and lands at the caster instead of sliding toward the opponent.
Camera cuts follow the demonstration's sequence but are rebuilt for our arena
and viewport; they are NOT imported Unity camera curves or a 1:1 camera port.
