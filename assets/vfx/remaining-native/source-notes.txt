Original HI3 FK Artifact / NL Dress01 ultimate resources

Textures and meshes are original game artwork, not repainted substitutes.

Active catalog: 34 emitter rows, 14 source meshes.
- Avatar_Fuka_FK_AF01_Ultra: 23 rendered layers, augmented Hawk of the Fog.
- Avatar_Fuka_NL_Attack_UltraThunder_Dress01: 3 layers.
- Avatar_Fuka_NL_Ultra_Follow_Dress01: 4 layers.
- Avatar_Fuka_NL_Attack_UltraThunder: 1 layer.
- Avatar_Fuka_NL_Ultra_Follow: 3 layers.
NL UltraThunder is a secondary arc emitter, not the complete transformation.
Its source-texture shockwave composition remains, with corrected original
circle/lightning additive and trail/impact alpha emission material values.

FK base-suit C2_3_Ultra and QYHY follow were previously assigned to AF01.
They have been removed from the active catalog: they belong to a different suit.
AF01's .6-second source impact is aligned to the visible landing clip's
.4701334 contact by a .1298666 cue offset, preserving relative emitter delays.
Reference: official Hawk of the Fog tutorial at
https://bh3.mihoyo.com/gl/character/detail/1119

Rows record texture hashes, original material values, source shader identity
and hash, emitter modules, transform chains, and renderer mesh references.
Mesh arrays retain original vertices, UVs, indices, available colors/normals.
Renderer mesh PPtrs account for variable vertex-stream length (344/348 bytes).
The trail meshes are Fuka_trail01, not generic quads. Built-in ID 10210 is a quad.

Material fixes are based on original shader metadata and DXBC inspection:
- Particle_Channel_Mix_New and Layer1/Channel Mix interpolate colors with R/G;
  their coverage channel is B. These channels are not summed as colored light.
- Legacy particle material tint is multiplied by two; .5 is the neutral value.
- Retain _EmissionScaler, alpha/additive families, texture scrolling and mask.
- Keep vertex alpha, separate-axis size curves, angular velocity, velocity-over-
  lifetime, and texture-sheet frames. Pause uses the simulation clock throughout.

Web adaptation: release scale .85, follow scale 1.0; explicit local texture halos
replace screen HDR bloom. Scene-only dimming leaves characters readable. The
fog camera pulls back for its native circular burst. Shared live-layer cap:112.
DLCSmoke now retains its original _DisTexture and threshold; lifetime supplies
the missing custom-stream dissolve bias, with an explicit Web cone-edge feather.
This is not a complete Unity renderer: distortion, native trail simulation,
DLCSmoke custom vertex streams and full custom-data shading still differ.

Rebuild from local exports with tools/import_native_ultimates.py
 --source-root <exports directory> --suit fog (or shadow), then
 tools/prepare_native_effect_materials.py --source-root <exports directory>.
